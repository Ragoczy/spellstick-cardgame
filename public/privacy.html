Spellstick privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Playing against the computer needs no account, and nothing about you is stored. This page is about signing in to play online.

What we get from Discord

When you sign in with Discord, Discord tells us your Discord user ID, username, and avatar, and whether you are in our Discord server, when you joined it, and which of its roles you have. We don't get your email address, password, messages, or other servers.

What we keep

  • Your Discord user ID, username, and avatar, so we know it's you next time.
  • When your Discord account was made and when you joined our server, to stop throwaway accounts from abusing trading and starter cards later.
  • The team or manager name you choose. Other players see this name, not your Discord name.
  • When you last signed in, and a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 7 days.

We use Discord's sign-in permission only while you sign in, then discard it. We don't keep it, and we can't post or read anything as you.

Who sees it

Only you, and the game's moderators when they need to settle a problem. We don't sell or share it, and there are no ads or trackers.

Deleting your account

Ask a moderator on our Discord server, and we'll delete your account and everything stored about it.
