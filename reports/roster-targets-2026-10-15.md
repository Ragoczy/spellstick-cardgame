# Roster targets, 2026-10-15

Made by `npm run roster-targets` (PLAN.md, Phase f). 20 team pairs per target, each played on 250 seeds with the teams both ways round (10000 games per target). Both teams use Team A's 16 spells. Scores count a win as 1 and a draw as ½, with a 95% interval. The team cap is off for these games, so the dearer teams can go over it.
