## Findings (written by Claude after reading the numbers)

These are the rules Paul adopted on 2026-10-06: two actions per turn, the decks as the game
clock, a penalty shootout for ties, tied passes caught by the receiver, and goalies at Save 3/2.

- **Balance targets are met:** first player 49.3%, Team A 49.9%, 1.62 goals per game,
  14.6% draws (Paul accepted about 15%). With 3 lanes: 51.7%, 49.9%, 1.58 goals, 11.2% draws.
- **Turns (25) are below 30–50, but each turn has two actions.** That's about 50 actions,
  similar table time to the old target. Check the real time in the first playtest before tuning.
  Going back to one action per turn would fix the count but halve the scoring (0.95 goals).
- **A big penalty bonus makes things worse.** At +2, almost everyone scores, so shootouts run
  out of shooters and draws double to 28%. Leave it at 0.
- **Safe catch** would raise scoring to 2.07 goals per game. Keep it in reserve in case
  playtesters find games too low-scoring.
- **Cards that never or rarely get used** (for the content pass, M5):
  - The backup goalie (Save 2): nobody swaps a Save 3 goalie for a worse one.
  - Swap: never cast.
  - Scry: rarely cast.
