## Findings (written by Claude after reading the numbers)

Rules v0.5 adds hits, dirty plays, Mend, and injuries. Nothing below has been applied.

**The injury system works, but hitting the goalie is the dominant strategy.**
- Goalies are only Save 3/2, so a hit (strength 3, or 4 on an affinity match) almost always lands
  on them. Hits land 90% of the time overall.
- A goalie hit twice is carried off. Teams rarely hold a spare goalie, so the goal then sits
  empty and any shot scores: 2.44 goals (and shootout penalties) per game go into an empty goal.
- That's where most of the scoring now comes from. Weaken hits (strength 2) or toughen goalies
  (Save +1) and goals drop from 1.91 to about 0.6 per game.
- Results: injuries 4.96 per game (target 2–4), and the more-injured team wins only 31.6%
  (target ≥ 35%), because losing your goalie usually loses the game.

**Nearly every carry-off is a goalie.** With "Goalies stay on" (a goalie injured a second time
keeps playing hurt instead of being carried off), carry-offs fall from 1.40 to 0.12 per game,
empty-goal goals go to 0, games with an empty spot fall from 82% to 4.5%, and the more-injured
team wins 40.3% of the time. Injuries are 4.01 per game, just over the target.

### Proposed tuning (needs Paul's OK)

1. **Goalies can't be carried off** (rule): a second injury on a goalie has no further effect.
2. **Two hits per team instead of three** (card data): the third hit becomes a Neutral Boost.

Together (1,000 games): **3.04 injuries per game ✅, more-injured team wins 36.4% ✅, 1.61 goals
per game ✅, 6.4% draws ✅**, first player 48.9% ✅. Team A wins 43.4% of decided games, which
is a placeholder-deck balance issue for the content pass (M5), not an injury problem.

Turns (24) are below 30–50 again, but each turn has two actions; check table time in the playtest.

- **Hit strength 2:** Hits have strength 2 instead of 3 (match 3, opposed 1).
- **Two hits per team:** Each team's third hit becomes a Neutral Boost.
- **Milder injuries:** Every injury takes off 1 point instead of 2 (Concussion stays −1 to all).
- **Goalies Save +1:** Goalies at Save 4 and 3, so they resist hits (and shots) better.
- **Goalies stay on:** A goalie can't be carried off: a second injury on a goalie has no further effect.
- **Two hits + goalies stay on:** Both changes together.
- **Two hits + milder injuries:** Both changes together.
