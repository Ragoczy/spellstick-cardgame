# Simulation report — 2026-10-07

1000 games, computer vs computer, seed 1, 2 lanes, card set 0.5-prototype, goals to win 3. Took 73 s.

Generated by `npm run sim`. Nothing in this report has been applied to the rules or cards.

## Findings (written by Claude after reading the numbers)

Rules v0.5 with Paul's ruling that **hitting the goalie isn't allowed** (goalies can't be
injured by hits or dirty plays). Nothing below has been applied.

**The injury system now meets its targets:** 3.04 injuries per game (target 2–4), and the
more-injured team still wins 51% of decided games (target ≥ 35%). Carry-offs are 0.33 per game,
an empty spot appears in 17% of games, and no goals go into an empty goal.

**But scoring dropped to 0.52 goals per game** (target 1–2; it was 1.62 before injuries), and
draws rose to 22%. The cause is the deck change, not injuries: each team lost four of its five
boosts, and boosts are what win passes and shots. Turning one hit back into a boost already lifts
goals to 0.78.

### Proposed change (needs Paul's OK): keep the boosts, drop Scry and Long Shot instead

Per team: three hits, Late Hit, and Mend replace Earth Swap, the two Scry, Fire Long Shot, and
one boost (instead of four boosts). Scry and Long Shot were the least-used spells before this
change. Tested separately, 1,000 games each:

| | Goals/game | Draws | First player wins | Team A wins | Injuries/game | More-injured team wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 lanes | 1.37 ✅ | 13.6% ✅ | 48.8% ✅ | 45.5% ✅ | 3.12 ✅ | 48.8% ✅ |
| 3 lanes | 1.40 ✅ | 12.5% ✅ | 49.9% ✅ | 48.8% ✅ | 3.11 ✅ | 52.1% ✅ |

Every target is met except turns (25–27), which is expected with two actions per turn; check table
time in the playtest. With two hits per team instead of three, goals are 1.44 and injuries 2.31.

- **Two hits + milder injuries:** Both changes together.
