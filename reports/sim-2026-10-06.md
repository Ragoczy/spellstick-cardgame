# Simulation report — 2026-10-06

Generated by `npm run sim`. Nothing in this report has been applied to the rules or cards.

## Findings (written by Claude after reading the numbers)

**1. Goals are far too rare for "first to 3 goals wins."** Teams average 0.36 goals and 1 shot per
game, and no game reached 3 goals. Two out of three games end in a draw after sudden death.
The cause is structural, not one bad number:

- To score, you must catch a pass at forward (about 1 in 2 succeed), survive the defender's tackle
  on their turn, then beat the goalie (about 1 in 3). That works out to roughly 1 attack in 10.
- Holding the ball in midfield is safe: tackles fail 73% of the time and a failed tackle costs
  nothing. So the ball mostly sits there.
- This isn't just a timid computer player. An all-out version that always shoots when it can and
  otherwise always passes forward scored *fewer* goals (0.48 per game, versus 0.36 for the normal
  player), because most of its passes were intercepted.

Even the best experiments only reach about 1 goal per game. Reaching "first to 3" inside 30–50
turns would need roughly 4 times that, so number tweaks alone won't get there.

**2. Team B wins 59% of decided games.** Its placeholder deck has many Defense 6 players. This is
worth fixing when the real rosters are made (M5); it isn't a rules problem.

**3. First-player advantage is fine** (52.6%).

**4. Some spells are almost never worth casting:** Swap (never), Scry and Long Shot (rarely).
Shield is very strong (the caster's side wins about 85% of the time).

### Recommendation (needs Paul's decision; nothing has been changed)

- **Make the deck the game clock.** The match ends when the cards run out, and the team with more
  goals wins, like full time in a real match. Then 1–2 goals per game is fine, the way real low-scoring sports work.
  Replace the long sudden death with a quick **penalty shootout** (for example, each team picks a forward to shoot at
  the goalie, best of 3). That ends draws, and it's a guaranteed "did that just happen?" moment.
- **Make scoring easier**, using the best-tested changes:
  - *Goalies Save −1* (5/4 → 4/3): doubles goals, a simple card change.
  - Plus either *Two actions per turn* (1.05 goals, 33 turns) or *Safe catch for forwards* (0.91 goals, 46 turns).
    Two actions per turn is the bigger change to how a turn feels; safe catch is one extra rule line.
- **Swap the "first to 3" target** in the roadmap for "about 1–2 goals per game, under 10% draws."
- Rework Swap and Scry, or replace them, in the content pass.

- **Win at 2 goals:** First to 2 goals wins instead of 3.
- **Goalies Save −1:** Goalie cards have Save 4 and 3 instead of 5 and 4.
- **Defense max 5:** No field player has Defense above 5 (the Defense 6 stoppers become 5).
- **Tied passes caught:** A tied pass goes to the receiver instead of the interceptor.
- **Safe catch (forwards):** A forward who catches a pass can't be tackled until their team's next turn, so they always get a chance to shoot.
- **Safe catch + Save −1:** Safe catch for forwards, and goalies have Save 4 and 3.
- **Two actions per turn:** Each turn you take 2 actions instead of 1 (for example, pass and then shoot). A goal ends your turn.
- **Two actions + Save −1:** Two actions per turn, and goalies have Save 4 and 3.
- **Safe catch + Save −1 + 2 goals:** All three together: safe catch for forwards, goalies Save 4 and 3, first to 2 goals.
