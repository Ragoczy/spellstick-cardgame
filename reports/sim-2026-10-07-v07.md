"Upsets": contests where the weaker printed card (stat, ability, injury) won anyway, thanks to spells or dice.

- **Goalies Save 5:** Weaker goalies: more shots score.
- **Goalies Save 7:** Tougher goalies (penalties +4 to keep shootouts short).
- **Pass bonus +1:** The receiver adds 1 to their Speed in every pass instead of 2.
- **Regroup 2 cards:** Regroup swaps up to 2 cards (the rule before v0.7): shorter games.
- **No Regroup:** Regroup only skips the action: longer games, more shots.

## Findings (written by Claude after reading the numbers)

Rules v0.7 is the "hockey feel" package Paul chose from the balance review (see
sim-2026-10-07-hockey.md): pass +2, rolled shots, goalies Save 6, penalties +3, Regroup 1 card,
and balanced rosters.

- About 9 passes in 10 are caught, and about 1 shot in 4 scores. Every target is met except
  injuries (4.1 a game, just over 4). With 3 lanes, turns are 28.8, just under 30.
- About 40% of games go to a penalty shootout. That's the cost of goalies stopping most shots
  with about 6 shots a game.
