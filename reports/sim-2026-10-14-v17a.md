# Rules v0.17, phase a: Shield cap

Plan: `PLAN.md`, "Phase a". Every run plays the same seeds as the run it's compared with, so the
deals and the first player match, and every number has a 95% interval.

## Decision

**A Shield takes 2 off the opposing player's value** (not below 0), worked out after abilities
and injuries; dice and spells are added after. Setup also draws **3 spells instead of 2**, because
games ran just over the 50-turn limit.

## How the cap was chosen

The plan's rule: try half (rounded up), −3, and −2 in that order, and keep the first that passes
all three checks.

| Check | Half, rounded up | −3 | −2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Unanswered Shield scores under 75%: Shot 5 vs Save 6 | 72.2% | 72.2% | 58.3% |
| 1. Same, averaged over every shot taken | 64.7% | 71.0% | 57.7% |
| 2. Shield's win edge no higher than an open +2 Boost's | +21.4 vs +20.5 (no, within luck) | +22.0 vs +20.3 (no) | **+19.0 vs +20.4 (yes)** |
| 3. Goals within ±25% of v0.16 (2.39 on these seeds) | 2.31 | 2.30 | 2.25 |

Half missed check 2 by 0.9 points of win edge, about one standard error, so it's a near miss
rather than a clear failure. −2 is the first cap that passes everything. Switching to half later
is a one-line config change (`shieldEffect: 'half'`).

The win edge is measured the `npm run spell-tiers` way: the test team swaps four rarely-cast
spells for four copies of the spell, 2,000 games per spell, against a control with no swap. The
Shield edge is the average over all 13 shields, and the Boost edge over the 3 open +2 boosts.
In v0.16 the Shield's edge was +24.0 and the Boost's +18.4.
