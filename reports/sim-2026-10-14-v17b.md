# Rules v0.17, phase b: Toughness, knockdowns, and severity

Plan: `PLAN.md`, "Phase b". Phase a rules (Shield −2, 3 setup spells) against the same rules plus
contact, on the same 5,000 seeds. Every number has a 95% interval; the full tables follow.

## What changed

- **Toughness** on every field player, by type: Stoppers and Anchors 5, Playmakers and
  All-rounders 4, Runners and Strikers 3.
- **A successful tackle knocks the holder down:** a roll above their Toughness hurts them.
  Hits and dirty plays hurt with no Toughness roll. A player is hurt at most once per contest.
- **Severity roll** for every hurt: 1–3 Shaken, 4–5 Injured (an injury card, as before), 6
  Knocked out.
- **Shaken:** can't pass, shoot, tackle, or cast, −2 in contests, stands up at the end of their
  team's next turn (or when substituted out, or at full time).
- **Knocked out:** beside the mat; a forced substitution; back to the hand on a 4–6 at a faceoff.
- The computer values a hurt by its chance of each severity, counts the knockdown chance in a
  tackle (guessing a face-down player's Toughness by row), and treats Shaken players as weaker.

## Notes

- **Hurts are under the plan's estimate** (3.55 against about 4.5). Knockdowns came in close to
  the estimate, but the computer casts fewer hits (2.81 → 2.35) and dirty plays (1.00 → 0.57) a
  game. A hurt is now half Shaken, and there are fewer already-injured players to finish off, so
  those spells look less worthwhile to it. The plan expected the low end here; phase e has the
  levers (tackle assist, tackle risk), and phase c2 adds physical attacks.
- **Real injuries fell** (2.87 → 1.75), because half of all hurts are now only Shaken and
  carry-offs almost vanished (0.24 → 0.01). This is the "too few injuries" target, which the plan
  meets mainly with physical attacks in phase c2 (estimate: about +1.35 real injuries a game).
  That alone would reach about 3.1, still under 3.5, so expect to use a lever: the plan's own
  fallback order for knockouts is the return roll, and the severity table can lean further toward
  Injured.
- **Spells cast fell** with the hits and dirty plays above, plus Shaken players who can't cast.
- Phase c changes dirty plays (declared before the dice, with a referee), so their numbers will
  move again there.

# Comparison: Phase b, 2026-10-14

Made by `npm run sim-compare`: 5000 computer-vs-computer games per run (seed block 1), 2 lanes, card set 0.16-prototype. Took 84 s on 8 workers.

- Before: config changes `knockdowns=false`; cards data/cards.prototype.json.
- After (Phase b): config changes `knockdowns=true`; cards data/cards.prototype.json.

Every result has a 95% interval: if the same run were repeated with new seeds, the result would land in that range 19 times out of 20. Both runs play the same seeds (the same deals and first player), and each change is measured seed by seed, so a small real change still shows. A target is "Yes" when the whole interval is inside it, "No" when the whole interval is outside it, and "Borderline" when luck could put it either side.
