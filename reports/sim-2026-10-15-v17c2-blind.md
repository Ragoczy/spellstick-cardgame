# Trial: blind physical draw (Paul's idea, 2026-10-15)

Physical cards aren't in the hand, so they don't count toward the 7-card limit. Each team's
Physical pile stays face down, and an attack flips the top card: the move is decided by the player
on the field in the moment. Switched on with `physicalDraw: 'blind'` (in a game against the
computer, add `?physical=blind` to the address). The default is still cards in hand.

Claude's calls for the trial: a flipped card's own condition is ignored (Clothesline on a player
without the ball, Stamp on a standing player, Hooking's other lane); Late Hit and Elbow are plain
attacks, since with no hand there's nothing to react with; nothing is drawn at setup or faceoffs.
The computer values a blind attack as the average over the cards left in its pile (which cards are
left is public; their order isn't).

Against cards in hand (phase c2), same seeds. The limit is a second what-if: each team can flip
only so many cards a game, with no reshuffle (`physicalAttackLimit`).

## What it shows

- **With no card to spend, attacks are almost free,** and an attack on the ball carrier (Speed
  against Speed, plus the card) beats a tackle (Defense against Speed). Unlimited, the computer
  attacks 19 times a game, mostly at the ball carrier, and stops hitting (1.63 → 0.43 hits a game).
- **It does what the rule change wanted:** real injuries reach the 3.5 target for the first time.
- **But goals and spells pay for it.** Every attack is an action not spent passing, shooting, or
  casting. Somewhere between 6 and 10 attacks per team, goals cross below their floor (1.84) as real
  injuries cross above theirs (3.5).
- **Spells cast stay under target in every version,** blind or not: attacks compete with spells for
  the same action.

# Comparison: Blind physical draw, 2026-10-15

Made by `npm run sim-compare`: 5000 computer-vs-computer games per run (seed block 1), 2 lanes, card set 0.16-prototype. Took 217 s on 8 workers.

- Before: config changes none; cards data/cards.prototype.json.
- After (Blind physical draw): config changes `physicalDraw=blind`; cards data/cards.prototype.json.

Every result has a 95% interval: if the same run were repeated with new seeds, the result would land in that range 19 times out of 20. Both runs play the same seeds (the same deals and first player), and each change is measured seed by seed, so a small real change still shows. A target is "Yes" when the whole interval is inside it, "No" when the whole interval is outside it, and "Borderline" when luck could put it either side.
