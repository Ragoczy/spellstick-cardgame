# Rules v0.17, phase c2: physical attack cards and the penalty box

Plan: `PLAN.md`, "Phase c2" (decisions 8, 9, and 11). Phase c rules against the same rules plus
physical attacks, on the same 5,000 seeds. Every number has a 95% interval; the full tables follow.

## What changed

- **16 physical cards** (Shirt Pull, Tripping, Hooking, Atomic Wedgie, Slashing, Cross-check,
  Clothesline, Gouge, Slew-foot, Late Hit, Elbow, Kneecap, Spearing, Chop Block, Stamp, Pile-on),
  now real card data with their own card type and a small vocabulary of effects.
- Each team has a **Physical pile** of all 16: draw 2 at setup and 1 at each faceoff. Physical
  cards share the hand (and its 7-card limit) with players and spells.
- **An attack is an action:** Speed (Defense for muscle cards) plus the card's bonus and +1 for
  its body Affinity, against the target's Speed. A win makes the target drop the ball and knocks
  them down with the card's modifiers. Late Hit and Elbow are reactions.
- **The penalty box:** a 1 on the attacker's die, then a 1 on the penalty die, sends the attacker
  off for 1, 2, or 3 turns by tier. The spot stays empty, nobody can come on there, and a goal by
  the other team brings them back.
- The computer attacks when the chance of winning, times the ball and the hurt, beats the small
  penalty risk and the card.

## Notes

- **Real injuries are still short** (2.87 against 3.5; the plan estimated about 3.7 here). Half of
  all hurts are only Shaken, and the computer casts fewer hits (1.60 → 1.13 hurt a game), since an
  attack now competes for the same action.
- **Spells cast fell by 2.8 a game.** Two causes: physical cards take hand slots, so hands are
  often full (a full hand throws away the top card at the draw step instead of drawing), and
  attacks replace some spell actions.
- **Games got longer** (+3.7 turns), past the 50-turn limit. More hurts mean more substitutions at
  the Draw step, and a substitution draws no card, so the piles (the game clock) run down slower.
- **Bug found and fixed by the simulator:** an attacker hurt by their own Spearing who was then
  penalized left a forced substitution waiting, which put someone in the penalized spot. A penalty
  now cancels it (they go to the box injured).

# Comparison: Phase c2, 2026-10-15

Made by `npm run sim-compare`: 5000 computer-vs-computer games per run (seed block 1), 2 lanes, card set 0.16-prototype. Took 166 s on 8 workers.

- Before: config changes `physicalAttacks=false`; cards data/cards.prototype.json.
- After (Phase c2): config changes `physicalAttacks=true`; cards data/cards.prototype.json.

Every result has a 95% interval: if the same run were repeated with new seeds, the result would land in that range 19 times out of 20. Both runs play the same seeds (the same deals and first player), and each change is measured seed by seed, so a small real change still shows. A target is "Yes" when the whole interval is inside it, "No" when the whole interval is outside it, and "Borderline" when luck could put it either side.

No crashes, no impossible states, and no game hit the turn cap.
