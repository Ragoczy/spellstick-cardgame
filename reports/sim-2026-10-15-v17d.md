# Rules v0.17, phase d: healing, Ward, and player abilities

Plan: `PLAN.md`, "Phase d" (decisions 3, 4, 5). The card data before phase d against the card data
after, with the same engine and the same 5,000 seeds (blind physical draw, 8 per team, escalating
penalties). Every number has a 95% interval; the full tables follow.

## What changed

- **Mend** (Mend, Knit, Set the Bone) can also bring one of your knocked-out players back to your
  hand. In the game, Mend shows a "Bring back" button for each knocked-out player.
- **Ward** (new reaction effect): if your player is hurt in this contest, they're only Shaken.
  Ward and Stone Skin become wards; Team A swaps Scrying Bowl for Stone Skin, Team B swaps True
  Sight for Hex. Both decks: 1 boost, 2 shields, 1 ward, 1 dirty play, 3 hits, 2 steals, 1 long
  shot, 1 long pass, 1 decoy pass, 1 mirror images, 1 recall, 1 mend.
- **Player abilities**, 8 named players each, picked by Resonant with a fixed seed
  (`npm run assign-abilities`):
  - Heavy hands (`hits_harder`): anyone they hurt rolls 1 higher for severity.
  - Sturdy (`hard_to_hurt`): they roll 1 lower when hurt.
  - Quick but fragile: +2 Speed when evading a tackle or physical attack, Toughness 1 lower.
- The computer plays Ward when its player is likely to be hurt (about 1.2 times a game) and uses
  Mend on knocked-out players (about 0.25 times a game).

## Notes

- **Real injuries moved further from target** (3.44 → 3.24): a Ward turns an injury or knockout
  into Shaken, and Sturdy and Quick but fragile players are harder to hurt. That's the point of
  Ward, so the injury target now rests on phase e's levers (tackle assist, tackle risk).
- **Spells cast rose by 2.2** because the computer plays its Ward; that recovers most of what the
  physical attacks took.
- **Found while checking:** at first the computer never cast Ward, because it counted any reaction
  spell as costing a full card. Since a Ward has no other use, it now counts as a cheap card.
- **For phase f:** `npm run spell-tiers` swaps out four rarely-cast Team A spells, and one of them
  (Scrying Bowl) is no longer in Team A's deck; the list needs updating before measuring spells.

# Comparison: Phase d, 2026-10-15

Made by `npm run sim-compare`: 5000 computer-vs-computer games per run (seed block 1), 2 lanes, card set 0.16-prototype. Took 216 s on 8 workers.

- Before: config changes none; cards reports/.phase-c2-cards.json.
- After (Phase d): config changes none; cards data/cards.prototype.json.

Every result has a 95% interval: if the same run were repeated with new seeds, the result would land in that range 19 times out of 20. Both runs play the same seeds (the same deals and first player), and each change is measured seed by seed, so a small real change still shows. A target is "Yes" when the whole interval is inside it, "No" when the whole interval is outside it, and "Borderline" when luck could put it either side.
