# Rules v0.17, phase e: optional tackle rules

Plan: `PLAN.md`, "Phase e". Paul's instruction: try tackle assist and tackle risk, and keep
whichever moves real injuries toward 3.5 without pushing another target out. Each option ran
against phase d on the same 5,000 seeds (blind physical draw, 8 per team, escalating penalties).

## The options

- **Tackle assist:** +1 to a tackle for each of the tackler's standing teammates (on the field, not
  Shaken) one lane over in the same row.
- **Tackle risk:** a tackler who loses by 3 or more is Shaken (no Toughness or severity roll).

**Kept: tackle risk.** It moves real injuries toward 3.5 (a little: +0.05, 95% interval +0.00 to
+0.09) and pushes no target out; spells cast even rise, since the computer tackles less often but
better. **Assist** raises real injuries more, but drops goals below their 1.84 floor, so it's not
kept (it stays as a what-if switch, `tackleAssist`). Both together push goals and total hurts out.

## The gap that's left

Real injuries are at 3.28 against 3.5. A what-if with the severity roll at 1–2 Shaken (instead of
1–3), on top of tackle risk, shows the trade (`sim-2026-10-15-v17e-severity.md`):

| Severity table | Real injuries | Shaken share | Hurts | Goals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 Shaken (the rule) | 3.28 | 58.1% | 7.84 | 1.91 |
| 1–2 Shaken | **4.33** | **46.7%** ✗ | **8.13** ✗ | 1.91 |

On a six-sided die there's no step between the two tables, so "at least 3.5 real injuries" and "at
least half Shaken" can't both be met by the severity table alone. That's a call for Paul.
