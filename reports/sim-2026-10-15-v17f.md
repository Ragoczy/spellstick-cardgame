# Rules v0.17, phase f: player costs and the team cap

*Superseded prices:* after this, forced substitutes coming on Shaken became the rule and the
players were re-priced under it (40% by type instead of half). The method here still stands; the
current numbers are in `sim-2026-10-15-v17g.md`, "Made the rule".

Plan: `PLAN.md`, "Phase f". Every named player gets a **cost**, and a built team's players may cost
at most the **team cap**. Costs come only from measured values, never from how often a skill is
used.

unchanged team. Each of 5,000 seeds is played twice, sides swapped, for 10,000 games per change.
The value per point is the edge in points per 100 games, divided by the cards that got the change.

## By type group

The flat values above priced a Defense point on a Stopper the same as on a Striker, and
Defense-heavy teams lost at equal cost (below). Paul: price each stat by type. A second run

group's players. The groups are Runners and Strikers, Playmakers and All-rounders, and Anchors and

A stat is worth most on the types that lean on it. Shot on a defender is worth little.

**Kept: half and half.** Each type group's value is the average of its by-type value and the flat
value. It's the only pricing of the three that meets both roster targets (below). Pure by-type
prices overshoot: they make defenders so cheap that a Defense-heavy team can afford two Save-6
goalies and better middle players, and it then wins 58.5%.

## The formula

A player's cost is the base plus the scale times what they're worth above the weakest value of
each part in the pool. For example, Speed 5 is 2 points above the pool's lowest Speed of 3. The
cheapest field player costs 6, and the average field player and the average goalie cost about 10.
`npm run price-players` (half and half is its default) writes the values to `data/player-values.json` and
the costs into the card data. The tests check every cost against the formula.

Claude: costs count from the pool's weakest values, not from zero. The measurements say what one
*more* point is worth. Counting from zero would price a goalie at about 70 next to a field
player's 10.

| | Costs |
| --- | --- |
| Field players | 6 to 15, average 9.9 |
| By type | Runner 11.9, Striker 11.9, All-rounder 9.7, Playmaker 9.4, Stopper 8.3, Anchor 8.1 |
| Goalies | Save 5: 5 or 6. Save 6: 21 or 22. |
| Starter set | 220 |
| **Team cap** | **238** (an average 24-player team) |

The goalie gap is what the measurement says: one point of Save on both goalies is worth about as
much as one point of Speed on all 22 field players.

## Roster targets

From `npm run roster-targets` (`roster-targets-2026-10-15.md`): 20 team pairs × 250 seeds × 2
sides, 10,000 games each, with the cap off so a dearer team can exceed it. For Defense-heavy vs
Striker-heavy, each team picks its own goalies and 8 middle players and the totals match within
1%. Claude: the plan had the two teams share their goalies and middle players. With prices by type
that can't reach equal cost: the dearest 14 defenders cost 129 and the cheapest 14 attackers 152.
So the Defense-heavy team spends its savings on goalies and middle players instead. That's also
what a player under the cap would do.

| Pricing | Defense-heavy scores (want 45–55%) | Dearer team, 10% gap (want 55–65%) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat (first try) | 35.9% (35.0 to 36.9) ✗ | 62.0% (61.1 to 62.9) |
| By type | 58.5% (57.5 to 59.4) ✗ | 61.6% (60.7 to 62.6) |
| **Half and half (kept)** | **52.9% (51.9 to 53.8)** | **61.5% (60.6 to 62.5)** |

With the goalies shared, as first planned, flat pricing gave 40.5%.

## What needs Paul

**Being Shaken is worse than being Injured** when the team has a field player in hand. An Injured
player is swapped at once for a fresh player, for free. A Shaken player stays on the field, unable
to act, at −2. So Sturdy (which turns Injured into Shaken) hurts its owner, Heavy hands helps the
victim, wards measure about 0, and Toughness is worth almost nothing. Both abilities cost 0 for
now. This is a game-feel question. Possible fixes: a forced substitution costs something (for
example, the sub comes on Shaken), or a Shaken player can be swapped out for free.
