# Rules v0.17 trial: forced substitutes come on Shaken

Paul asked for a trial of a forced substitute coming on Shaken, then a re-measure of Sturdy, Heavy
hands, wards and Toughness. The question was whether they were worth nothing because an Injured
player is swapped at once for a fresh one, which made being Injured better than being Shaken.

The switch is `forcedSubShaken` in `src/engine/config.ts`. Paul then made it the rule: see "Made
the rule" at the end.
It covers every forced substitute: after an injury, a carry-off, a knockout, or a sending off. The
computer counts the Shaken substitute as part of what an injury costs.

less, partly because Shaken substitutes can't tackle.

## What it means

- **Wards now work.** With the trial, Ward and Stone Skin are about as strong as a steal (Uncommon).
- **Heavy hands, Sturdy and Toughness don't change.** So the substitute rule wasn't the whole
  reason. They shift how *badly* a player is hurt, and in these games that barely moves the
  result. Both abilities are a fraction of a point per card. Small changes in severity seem to
  matter little; how *often* players are hurt is what counts. Heavy hands may also push the
  computer into more attacks, and so more penalties, than it gains.
- **Prices don't change** if the trial is kept: the two abilities still cost 0 and Toughness
  stays near 0. If the trial becomes the rule, the full price measurement (about 1.5 hours)
  should be re-run under it, because it changes how the game plays.

## Made the rule (Paul), and re-priced

price measurement was re-run under it: 5,000 seeds × 2 per change (`value-2026-10-15-v17g.md`),
10,000 seeds × 2 per type-group change (`value-2026-10-15-v17g-by-type.md`), and the spell tiers.
