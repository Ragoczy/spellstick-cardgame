# Spell power tiers, 2026-10-15 (sub-shaken)

Only some spells (effects: ward), with config changes: forcedSubShaken=true. The tiers in the card data are unchanged.

Made by `npm run spell-tiers`: 1000 computer games per spell. The test team swaps four rarely-cast spells (Tailwind, Precognition, Mend, Blast Shot) for 4 copies of the spell; the other team keeps Team A's 16. "Edge" is how many more points in 100 games the test team scores than with no swap (a win is 1, a draw ½). The control team scored 49.5. Luck moves one spell's edge by about 1.8 points either way (one standard error); an effect's average over several spells is steadier, and an edge under about 2 is no clear edge at all.
